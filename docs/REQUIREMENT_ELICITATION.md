# Requirement elicitation record

The handout lists "requirement elicitation exercises" under CO1. This file is the plan, instruments and write-up template for that exercise. **No interviews are recorded here yet.** The findings sections stay marked `TEAM` until real people have answered. Do not fill them from assumption.

## 1. Stakeholders and technique

| Stakeholder | Technique | Target number | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| BMU students | Short questionnaire (5 minutes, Google Form or paper) | 20 or more | What they order, when, pain points, delivery needs, laptop and phone needs |
| Food vendors (Lazeez, Kathi Junction, Domino's, Greenox) | Structured interview (15 minutes) | 2 or more | Menu upkeep, stock, delivery to campus, how orders arrive today |
| University Admin / BMU staff | Structured interview (15 minutes) | 1 | Vendor approval, agreement checks, what the University must control |
| Laptop and phone shops (optional) | Short phone call | 1 or more | Whether they would join, and what they would want to publish |

Also record any existing source the team used: the SRS (`supr_srs.txt`), the menu files in `docs/menu-sources/`, and observation of how orders are placed today (for example WhatsApp or phone).

Ask for consent before recording names. Report results in aggregate and do not publish names or phone numbers of students.

## 2. Student questionnaire

1. How often do you order food from outside the campus mess? (daily / a few times a week / weekly / rarely)
2. Where do you order from today? (list)
3. How do you place those orders? (phone call / WhatsApp / delivery app / walk-in)
4. What is the biggest problem you face? (price, wait, finding menus, not knowing if they deliver to campus, trust, other)
5. How important is it to see up front whether a vendor delivers to campus? (1 to 5)
6. Would you use a BMU-reviewed vendor list? Why or why not?
7. Which would you prefer: pay online, or pay the vendor on delivery or pickup? (Supr does not collect payment.)
8. Which languages do you want the site in? (English / Hindi / both)
9. Have you needed a laptop or phone repair or purchase near campus? Did you know where to go?
10. Which three features matter most? (tick: menu search, dietary filter, favourites, order tracking, order history, notifications, vendor contact details)
11. Year of study and hostel or day scholar (for grouping only).

## 3. Vendor interview guide

1. What do you sell, and how often does your menu or price change?
2. How do you take orders now, and how many a day?
3. Do you deliver to BMU? To which gates or hostels? Is there a minimum order or a charge?
4. How do you track what is in stock today?
5. What would make you list on a platform like this? What would put you off?
6. Who would update the menu and stock?
7. Are you willing to supply a signed agreement or documents to BMU?
8. What would you want to see on a vendor dashboard?

## 4. University Admin interview guide

1. Which documents should a vendor provide, and who decides whether an agreement is valid?
2. How long should an agreement last, and how are renewals handled?
3. What reasons would lead to stopping a vendor?
4. What records must be kept, and for how long?
5. Who may create student accounts, and how should eligibility be checked?
6. Who would host and maintain the system after the course?
